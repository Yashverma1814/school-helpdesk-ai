OAKRIDGE ACADEMY
COMPREHENSIVE FEE SCHEDULE & REGULATIONS (SESSION 2026-2027)
Circular No: OA/FIN/2026-27/01
Release Date: 20th February 2026

1. ONE-TIME ENROLLMENT CHARGES (NEW ADMISSIONS):
- Registration Form & Prospectus Fee: INR 2,500
- Admission Enrolment Fee: INR 35,000 (Non-refundable)
- Refundable Security Deposit: INR 15,000

2. TUITION FEES (PAYABLE SEMI-ANNUALLY / 2 INSTALLMENTS PER YEAR):
- Early Years (Playgroup, Pre-K, KG): INR 90,000 per annum (Semester: INR 45,000)
- Primary Section (Grades 1 to 5): INR 1,20,000 per annum (Semester: INR 60,000)
- Middle Section (Grades 6 to 8): INR 1,45,000 per annum (Semester: INR 72,500)
- Senior Section (Grades 9 & 10 - IGCSE): INR 1,65,000 per annum (Semester: INR 82,500)
- IB Diploma / Senior Secondary (Grades 11 & 12): INR 1,95,000 per annum (Semester: INR 97,500)

3. PAYMENT DUE TIMELINES:
- Term 1 Fee (April to September): Due on or before 15th April 2026
- Term 2 Fee (October to March): Due on or before 15th October 2026

4. PENALTY CLAUSE & RE-ADMISSION:
- Late fine of INR 100 per calendar day is applied from 16th of the due month until the end of the month.
- Accounts that remain unpaid past the 30th of the due month will incur an additional administrative surcharge of INR 3,000.
- All payments must be settled through the Oakridge Parent Finance Gateway. Cash transactions are completely prohibited.
