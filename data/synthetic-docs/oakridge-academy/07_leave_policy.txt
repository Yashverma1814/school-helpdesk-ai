OAKRIDGE ACADEMY
STUDENT ABSENCE AND ATTENDANCE PROTOCOLS
Circular No: OA/POL/2026-09

1. ATTENDANCE BENCHMARK:
- Minimum mandatory attendance is 85% of total academic contact hours.
- Students whose attendance falls below 80% without sanctioned medical extenuation will not be eligible to write semester summatives.

2. SUBMISSION OF ABSENCE NOTIFICATIONS:
- Single Day Illness: Must be recorded on the Oakridge Parent Hub application before 08:00 AM on the day of illness.
- Anticipated Leaves (Weddings, Family Commitments): Must be requested at least 7 business days in advance to the Pastoral Care Head and is capped at a maximum of 3 days per semester.
- Medical Leaves (3+ days): Formal submission of diagnostic reports and physician clearance certificate is compulsory upon return.

3. PERSISTENT ABSENTEEISM:
- More than 5 consecutive unauthorized absent days triggers a formal parent hearing with the Dean.
- Extended unauthorized leave exceeding 10 consecutive days leads to revocation of the school enrollment contract.
