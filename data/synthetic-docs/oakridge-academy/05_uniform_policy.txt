OAKRIDGE ACADEMY
STUDENT ATTIRE & UNIFORM CODE (2026-2027)
Circular No: OA/STU/2026/04

1. FORMAL DAY DRESS (MONDAY & THURSDAY):
- Primary & Middle School: Crisp sky-blue collared shirt with charcoal grey tailored trousers (or charcoal culottes / pleated skirts), striped navy-gold silk tie, charcoal crew-length socks, and polished dark brown leather oxford shoes.
- Senior School (Grades 9 to 12): Charcoal grey tailored blazer with the school gold-wire crest, white formal shirt, tartan tie/cravat, and formal leather shoes.

2. CASUAL & ACTIVE WEAR (TUESDAY, WEDNESDAY, FRIDAY):
- Pique cotton polo shirt (House colors: Falcon Gold, Phoenix Crimson, Pegasus Azure, Orion Emerald).
- Navy tech-fleece shorts or joggers with breathable mesh paneling.
- Non-marking rubber-soled sports trainers (black, grey, or white only).

3. WINTER WARDROBE:
- Quilted charcoal bomber jacket with insulated fleece lining and engraved Academy brass buttons.
- Cashmere-blend navy V-neck cardigan for classroom hours.

4. PERSONAL PRESENTATION:
- Subtle, neat appearance is required. Smartwatches are permitted for Grades 9-12 solely in exam-free periods but prohibited during tests.
- Hair accessories must remain solid charcoal grey or black.
