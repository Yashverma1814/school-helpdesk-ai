OAKRIDGE ACADEMY
GLOBAL ADMISSIONS POLICY & CRITERIA (ACADEMIC YEAR 2026-2027)
Circular No: OA/ADM/2026-V1
Target Sections: Early Years, Middle Years Programme (MYP), Diploma Programme (DP)

1. AGE SPECIFICATIONS (CALCULATED AS OF 1ST JUNE 2026):
- Playgroup: 2.5 years completed
- Pre-Kindergarten: 3.5 years completed
- Kindergarten (KG): 4.5 years completed
- Grade 1: 5.5 years completed

2. ENROLMENT STAGES:
- Stage 1 (Digital Portfolio & Form): Registration fee of INR 2,500 submitted via apply.oakridge.example.edu.
- Stage 2 (Assessment & Diagnostics):
  * Early Years: Child observation session and parental interactive dialogue.
  * Grades 2 to 8: Online CAT4 cognitive abilities assessment (Spatial, Quantitative, Verbal, Non-verbal).
  * Grades 9 to 11: Subject aptitude assessment in Mathematics and English Literature followed by Dean's interview.
- Stage 3 (Acceptance & Seat Retention): Conditional offers released within 72 hours. Seat confirmation deposit due within 7 calendar days.

3. DIVERSITY & MERIT SCHOLARSHIPS:
- Academic Excellence Scholarship: Up to 25% tuition fee waiver for students scoring 95%+ in Grade 10 Board/IGCSE exams.
- Sports & Arts Scholarship: Up to 20% waiver for state or national medal holders.
