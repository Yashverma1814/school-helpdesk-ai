OAKRIDGE ACADEMY
ASSESSMENT & EXAMINATION MATRIX (2026-2027)
Circular No: OA/EXAM/2026-07
Controller of Academic Evaluations

1. ASSESSMENT STRUCTURE:
- Formative Milestones (Ongoing coursework, portfolio exhibits, project defenses): 40% weightage.
- Summative Examinations (Semester 1 & Semester 2 written papers): 60% weightage.

2. EXAMINATION DATES & TIMETABLES:
- Formative Diagnostic Review 1: 17th August 2026 to 24th August 2026
- Semester 1 Summative Exams (All Grades): 28th September 2026 to 12th October 2026
- Formative Diagnostic Review 2: 18th January 2027 to 25th January 2027
- Mock Diploma Examinations (Grades 10 & 12): 8th February 2027 to 20th February 2027
- Semester 2 Summative Final Exams: 15th March 2027 to 30th March 2027
- Final Academic Transcript Release: 9th April 2027

3. ASSESSMENT REGULATIONS:
- Students must attain a minimum grade point average of 3.0 (on a 4.0 scale) or 60% cumulative marks to advance into the next grade level.
- Absence during summative examinations requires a medical certificate endorsed by an accredited hospital board within 24 hours.
