GREENFIELD PUBLIC SCHOOL
ASSESSMENT SCHEME & EXAMINATION SCHEDULE (ACADEMIC YEAR 2026-2027)
Circular No: GPS/EXAM/2026/05
Issued by: Controller of Examinations

1. EVALUATION PATTERN:
- Primary Wing (Grades 1-5): Continuous & Comprehensive Evaluation (4 cycles per year, no formal midterm/final stress).
- Middle & Senior Wing (Grades 6-12):
  * Periodic Test 1 (PT-1): 40 Marks (Weightage: 10%)
  * Half-Yearly / Mid-Term Exams: 80 Marks (Weightage: 30%)
  * Periodic Test 2 (PT-2): 40 Marks (Weightage: 10%)
  * Annual Examination / Pre-Boards: 80 Marks (Weightage: 50%)

2. EXAMINATION DATES FOR SESSION 2026-2027:
- Periodic Test 1 (PT-1): 13th July 2026 to 20th July 2026
- Mid-Term Examination: 14th September 2026 to 28th September 2026
- Periodic Test 2 (PT-2): 7th December 2026 to 14th December 2026
- Pre-Board 1 (Grades 10 & 12): 4th January 2027 to 15th January 2027
- Pre-Board 2 (Grades 10 & 12): 1st February 2027 to 12th February 2027
- Annual Final Examinations (Grades 1-9 & 11): 24th February 2027 to 10th March 2027
- Annual Result Declaration & PTM: 26th March 2027

3. PASSING CRITERIA & RULES:
- Minimum 40% aggregate marks required in both theory and practical assessments independently.
- 75% minimum attendance is compulsory to sit for the Half-Yearly and Annual Examinations.
- Re-tests are permitted solely in cases of medical emergencies supported by a registered CMO certificate submitted within 48 hours.
