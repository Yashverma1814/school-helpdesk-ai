GREENFIELD PUBLIC SCHOOL
STUDENT ATTENDANCE & LEAVE APPLICATION POLICY
Circular No: GPS/LEAVE/2026/02
Applicability: Nursery through Grade 12

1. MINIMUM ATTENDANCE REQUIREMENT:
- Every student must maintain at least 75% overall attendance across the academic term.
- Students failing to meet 75% attendance without verified medical cause will be debarred from writing final exams.

2. LEAVE SANCTION PROCEDURE:
- Short Absence (1 to 2 days): Parents must enter the leave request through the Greenfield Parent Mobile App or send a written note in the student diary countersigned by the class teacher.
- Extended Absence (3 or more days): Requires a formal written application addressed to the Principal at least 48 hours in advance for prior sanction.
- Sick Leave: Medical certificates from a registered medical practitioner (MBBS/MD) are mandatory for absences exceeding 2 consecutive days, submitted on the day the student rejoins school.

3. UNAUTHORIZED LEAVE & CONTINUOUS ABSENCE:
- Continuous unexcused absence of more than 6 consecutive school days results in the student's name being struck off the rolls. Re-admission requires a fee of INR 3,000 subject to Principal's approval.
- Half-day leaves during school hours are discouraged and granted only on emergency grounds when parents personally arrive with valid photo ID.
