GREENFIELD PUBLIC SCHOOL
ANNUAL FEE STRUCTURE & PAYMENT SCHEDULE (SESSION 2026-2027)
Circular No: GPS/ADMIN/2026/01
Date: 15th March 2026

1. ADMISSION & REGISTRATION CHARGES (ONE-TIME):
- Registration & Prospectus: INR 1,500 (Non-refundable)
- Admission Fee (New Admissions only): INR 15,000 (Non-refundable)
- Security Caution Deposit: INR 5,000 (Refundable upon withdrawal)

2. ANNUAL TUITION FEES (PAYABLE QUARTERLY):
- Nursery & Kindergarten: INR 45,000 per annum (Quarterly Installment: INR 11,250)
- Primary School (Grades 1 to 5): INR 62,000 per annum (Quarterly Installment: INR 15,500)
- Middle School (Grades 6 to 8): INR 72,000 per annum (Quarterly Installment: INR 18,000)
- Secondary School (Grades 9 & 10): INR 78,000 per annum (Quarterly Installment: INR 19,500)
- Senior Secondary (Grades 11 & 12 - Science): INR 98,000 per annum (Quarterly Installment: INR 24,500)
- Senior Secondary (Grades 11 & 12 - Commerce/Arts): INR 92,000 per annum (Quarterly Installment: INR 23,000)

3. PAYMENT DUE DATES:
- Quarter 1 (April - June): Due by 10th April 2026
- Quarter 2 (July - September): Due by 10th July 2026
- Quarter 3 (October - December): Due by 10th October 2026
- Quarter 4 (January - March): Due by 10th January 2027

4. LATE PAYMENT PENALTIES & POLICIES:
- A late fine of INR 50 per day will be levied if payment is cleared between 11th and 25th of the due month.
- Defaults beyond the 25th of the due month incur INR 1,500 fixed penalty and temporary portal suspension.
- Mode of Payment: Fees must be paid exclusively via the Greenfield Parent ERP Portal or NetBanking.
- Cash payments above INR 2,000 are strictly not accepted at the school accounts desk.
