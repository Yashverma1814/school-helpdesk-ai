GREENFIELD PUBLIC SCHOOL
STUDENT UNIFORM & DRESS CODE REGULATIONS (2026-2027)
Circular No: GPS/DISC/2026/03

1. REGULAR SUMMER UNIFORM (MONDAY, TUESDAY, THURSDAY):
- Boys (Grades 1 to 10): White half-sleeved shirt with school monogram, navy blue shorts/trousers, navy blue belt with brass buckle, navy blue socks with twin white stripes, black leather lace shoes.
- Girls (Grades 1 to 10): White short-sleeved blouse with monogram, navy blue pleated skirt (knee length), navy blue socks, black buckled leather shoes, navy blue hair ribbon or hairband.
- Senior Boys & Girls (Grades 11 & 12): White formal shirt, grey check trousers / knee-length pleated skirts, maroon striped tie.

2. SPORTS / HOUSE UNIFORM (WEDNESDAY & SATURDAY):
- Respective House T-shirt (Red: Ruby, Blue: Sapphire, Green: Emerald, Yellow: Topaz).
- White track pants with green side piping and school monogram.
- All-white sports sneakers and white athletic cotton socks.

3. WINTER UNIFORM REGULATIONS (EFFECTIVE 1ST NOVEMBER):
- Maroon V-neck pullover with school monogram for Grades 1 to 8.
- Navy blue single-breasted blazer with embroidered crest mandatory for Grades 9 to 12.
- Full length grey woollen trousers for both boys and girls.

4. GROOMING & ACCESSORIES:
- Fancy watches, smartwatches, bracelets, dangling earrings, and nail polish are strictly prohibited.
- Hair must be neat, clean, and trimmed above collar for boys; girls with long hair must tie double braids with navy ribbons.
