GREENFIELD PUBLIC SCHOOL
ADMISSION POLICY & PROCEDURE (SESSION 2026-2027)
Circular No: GPS/ADM/2026/02
Target Grades: Nursery to Grade 9 & Grade 11

1. AGE CRITERIA (AS ON 31ST MARCH 2026):
- Nursery: Minimum 3 years complete
- Lower Kindergarten (LKG): Minimum 4 years complete
- Upper Kindergarten (UKG): Minimum 5 years complete
- Grade 1: Minimum 6 years complete

2. STEP-BY-STEP APPLICATION PROCEDURE:
- Step 1 (Online Registration): Parents submit application on www.greenfieldpublic.example.edu with registration fee of INR 1,500.
- Step 2 (Document Verification): Submission of self-attested copies of Birth Certificate, Aadhaar card of student & parents, recent passport photographs (4 nos), and previous school Transfer Certificate (TC) & Report Card.
- Step 3 (Interaction & Evaluation):
  * Nursery to Grade 2: Informal interaction with parents and child.
  * Grade 3 to Grade 9: Written proficiency assessment in English, Mathematics, and Science (duration: 90 minutes).
  * Grade 11: Admission based on Grade 10 Board cut-off (80%+ for Science stream, 75%+ for Commerce stream) plus interview.
- Step 4 (Offer & Fee Payment): Selected candidates receive an Offer of Admission via email with 5 working days validity to deposit admission fees.

3. SIBLING & ALUMNI PREFERENCE:
- 10% concession on tuition fees for the younger sibling studying concurrently.
- 5 bonus points awarded in nursery lottery for children of Greenfield alumni.

4. WITHDRAWAL & TC POLICY:
- One month advance written notice is mandatory for Transfer Certificate (TC) request.
- TC is processed within 7 working days post clearance of all library and accounts dues.
